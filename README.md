# RUN THE GAUNTLET

## Pitch 🥊
Run The Gauntlet is a fast-paced, first-person alternative controller cyberpunk boxing experience with one goal: fight your way through a gauntlet of enemy robots with a pair of gauntlets. 

Form unique combat combinations with a variety of punch attacks to defeat your robotic opponents. With a minute on the clock, you must perform precise attack sequences and time your blocks to take down as many robots and collect as much government scrap as possible.
